Questions

Is this medical advice?

No. A physician on our team reviews your plan and makes any medicine decision with you. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Will I get my medicine back?

Only if your physician agrees it is right for you. The goal is the lowest dose that works, and sometimes that is none.

Is regain after stopping normal?

Many people notice hunger, cravings or weight returning after they stop. It is not a lack of willpower. A plan helps you meet it.

Which medicines does it cover?

Semaglutide and tirzepatide first, as injections or pills.

What data do I need to share?

A 20 second daily check-in and your weekly weight. Labs, a wearable and body composition are optional.

What does it cost?

Joining is free and takes no payment. The planned price is $99 a month. Nothing is charged until we confirm the final price and you choose to start.

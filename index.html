    Illustrative figures.

    One step at a time. Always a way back.

    The goal is your lowest effective dose. Each change is small, reviewed and reversible.

    1. Baseline

      We record weight, waist, labs, blood pressure and hunger before anything changes.

    2. One step

      Your physician lowers the dose by one level.

    3. Hold for 4 to 8 weeks

      We watch hunger, weight and labs while your body adjusts.

    4. Decide together

      Step down, hold, or step back, using guardrails agreed in advance.

    Guardrails

    Examples. Your physician sets yours.

    Step down when

    • Weight is steady for about 3 weeks
    • Hunger is stable
    • Protein and strength targets are met

    Hold or step back when

    • Weight is up 2 to 3 kg over 4 weeks
    • Strong hunger or cravings return
    • Labs or blood pressure drift the wrong way

    Stopping with no plan usually ends in regain. Planned tapers are still being studied.

    • 14%

      People who switched from tirzepatide to placebo regained 14.0% of their body weight over the next 52 weeks. Those who continued lost a further 5.5%.

      SURMOUNT-4 trial, JAMA 2024
    • About two thirds

      One year after stopping semaglutide, people had regained about two thirds of the weight they had lost.

      STEP 1 extension, Diabetes, Obesity and Metabolism 2022
    • Early

      In one small study, 85 people with follow-up data held a stable weight 26 weeks after a 9 week taper with coaching. Only 85 of the 353 who tapered had data, so this is a hint, not proof.

      Embla taper study, reported by APhA

    Questions

    Is this medical advice?

    No. Offramp gives you a plan and tracks how your body responds. A physician on our team reviews it and makes dose decisions with you. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

    Who prescribes?

    A physician on our team reviews your plan and decides each dose change. Prescribing rules depend on where you live, so we confirm eligibility when we invite you.

    Which medicines does it cover?

    We are starting with semaglutide and tirzepatide, as injections or pills. Tell us what you take in the join form so we plan for it.

    Can I stop completely?

    Some people can. Many do best on the lowest dose that holds their weight. Your physician decides what is right for you. Coming down one step at a time matters because hunger tends to return quickly after an abrupt stop.

    What data do I need to share?

    Start with a 20 second daily check-in and your weekly weight. Labs, a wearable and body composition make the plan more personal and are optional.

    What does it cost?

    Joining the waitlist is free and takes no payment. We are still setting the price, and your answers in the join form help us set it fairly.

Questions

Is this medical advice?

No. A physician on our team reviews your plan and makes dose decisions with you. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Who prescribes?

A physician on our team decides each dose change. Rules depend on where you live, so we confirm eligibility when we invite you.

Which medicines does it cover?

Semaglutide and tirzepatide first, as injections or pills.

Can I stop completely?

Some people can. Many do best on the lowest dose that holds their weight. Your physician decides. Coming down one step at a time matters because hunger tends to return fast after an abrupt stop.

What data do I need to share?

A 20 second daily check-in and your weekly weight. Labs, a wearable and body composition are optional.

What does it cost?

Joining is free and takes no payment. We are still setting the price.
